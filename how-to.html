How to use this site

Scripture Games is a set of projector games for church classes. Open this folder’s index.html, or use the hosted copy. No install and no internet are required once the files are on the computer.

Play

  1. Pick a story, then a game, then Play.
  2. Jeopardy: choose teams → Start. Click a tile. Show Hint for the reference. Spacebar reveals the response.
  3. Pictionary: Next (or Spacebar) shows the next draw. Cover word so guessers watch the board, not the projector.
  4. Scripture Chase: click a hidden tile. Kids race to the reference. Reveal verse, then Discuss.
  5. Matching: flip two tiles (or use Face up for younger Primary). After a match, read the verse together (the reference opens Gospel Library). Shuffle to play again.

ESC usually goes back a step, then back to this hub.

Stories

Not every story has every game. The hub greys out games that are not in that pack. Isaiah: God Is My Salvation is Scripture Chase only. Isaiah: A Marvellous Work and a Wonder is Matching only.
